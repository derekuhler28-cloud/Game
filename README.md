# Pocket Pirate

A small pixel-art pirate life sim inspired by One Piece. You start as a nobody: no weapon, no powers, no crew, just the clothes on your back. Walk around each island and search its trash cans and shaking bushes for weapons and hidden Devil Fruits, master their moves, hire a crew at island bars, raid bosses, fix up your ship, sail over Reverse Mountain into the Grand Line and down to Fish-Man Island, awaken your Devil Fruit, and become Pirate King.

No build step and no dependencies: open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder with any static server). Progress saves automatically in your browser.

**Save codes**: open Stats and tap **Save code** to turn your whole save into one line of text (copy it somewhere safe). Paste it into **Load code** on the title screen, in any browser or on any device, to pick up right where you left off.

## Races

Pick one when you start a new game. Each changes your starting stats and has its own perks and flaws:

| Race | Perks | Flaws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | +25% mastery from everything | No racial move |
| Mink | +3 SPD, +2 ATK, Electro Claw move, +15% damage at night | Gets hungry 25% faster |
| Skypian | +10% dodge, +4 SP every turn, Impact Dial move | 15% less max HP |
| Fishman | +4 ATK, +15 max HP, Fishman Karate move (ignores defense), fishing pays 30% more | -2 SPD, -10% damage on Sandora and Cloud Isle |

Secret: tap the word "Choose" in the race picker's title. Every Devil Fruit circles the screen; pick one and it will be hidden on Dawn Village in your new game.

## How to play

Every island is a little town you walk around, old-handheld style. Use the on-screen **D-pad** (or arrow keys / WASD), or just tap where you want to go. **A** (Space, Enter or Z) talks, fights, searches, reads and goes inside; hold **B** (Shift or X) to run. Your crew follows you in a line.

- **Search**: dig through **trash cans** and **shaking bushes** for hidden weapons and the island's Devil Fruit, plus berries, food, supplies, ambushes and mentors. Each trash can can be searched once a day, and a few different bushes shake every morning. Every island's Devil Fruit hides in one particular bush or trash can that glows faintly in the fruit's colors: search it to find the fruit. The chest in the top corner shows how many secrets are left.
- **Clothes**: wear a hat, a coat and a charm, each with its own boost (stats, crit or dodge chance, less damage taken, extra SP, more XP, berries or mastery, or slower hunger). Every raid boss drops a signature piece the first time you beat them, shops sell some, and a few hide in trash cans and bushes. Put them on and take them off in Gear (or right in the shop); your pirate only changes looks when you choose to wear something. You can also hand clothes to crewmates at the Bar (Manage): since crewmates never get hit, on them a piece turns into attack power (its ATK, plus half its DEF and SPD and a tenth of its HP), adds its crit chance, and SPD or dodge makes them jump into normal fights more often. XP, berry, mastery and hunger perks on a crewmate still help you at half strength. Each piece can be worn by one person at a time, and kicking someone out gives you their clothes back.
- **Gear**: equip weapons and see every move you have unlocked or are working toward. Found fruits can be eaten (only one, ever), kept, or sold.
- **Mastery**: each weapon, your fists, and your Devil Fruit have their own mastery. Using them in fights, winning with them, and practicing with Drills raises mastery and unlocks their 2nd and 3rd moves.
- **Train**: walk up to a training dummy (or the weights on your ship). A timing mini-game: Stop the marker in the gold zone (dead center is PERFECT) to raise ATK, DEF/HP, SPD or Haki (each stat has a limit that rises with your level), or do Drills and Fruit Power practice for mastery.
- **Fight**: the island's enemies roam the edges of town where you can see them. Walk up to one and press A to pick a fight. Sometimes one spots you and charges (a "!" pops up): hold B to outrun it, or stand your ground. Turn-based battles for XP, berries and bounty. Beaten enemies come back after a while and every morning. Fights at night are tougher but pay more, and night prowlers charge more often.
- **Raid**: each island's boss stands guard outside their hideout at the north end of town. Talk to them to raid. Big multi-phase bosses. They charge a huge attack every few turns (Guard!), enrage at half HP, and your whole crew fights alongside you. Each boss drops its signature weapon, and some have a trick: Buggy splits apart so blades barely hurt him, Kuro dodges a lot (sure-hit moves, stuns and freezes pin him down), Don Krieg's armor blunts hits until it cracks at half HP (piercing moves get through), Smoker is made of smoke and his seastone jitte drains a Devil Fruit user's SP, Wapol swallows bullets and cannonballs, and every boss from Water 7 on has a trick of its own (see the table below). The raid screen lists each boss's trick before you fight.
- **Work**: grab the fishing rod at the end of the pier (tap REEL when the bobber dips), or take an odd job from the job board by the bar.
- **Bar**: walk in the door. The island's recruits sit at the tables: talk to one to hire them. Talk to the bartender (across the counter) to manage your crew or throw a feast. Every island has a bar with crewmates for hire (up to 5 at once). Hand each one a weapon you found, feed them a spare Devil Fruit from your bag (it's theirs for life), or kick them out (you get the weapon back; they leave with their fruit). Crewmates jump into normal fights sometimes and fight every turn in raids. Throw a feast at the bar to lift everyone's mood.
- **Bag / Sleep**: food, energy and mood drain as the day passes. Eat from your Bag and Sleep from the menu bar under the controls. Starving hurts; a good mood boosts training.
- **Shop**: walk into the building with the SHOP sign and talk to the shopkeeper. Food, medicine, Energy Cola, Water Barrels (soak Logia bosses), and ship upgrades, paint and figureheads. Each island sells different ones.
- **Boat**: your ship is moored at the pier on every island. Walk up the gangplank to go aboard; your crew hangs out on deck. The **map table** (or the helm) opens the sea chart; an island opens once you beat the raid boss before it. The **paint locker** picks your hull paint and figurehead, and lists every upgrade and where it's sold. Nap in the **hammock**, cook in the galley, lift the weights. The gangplank takes you back ashore.
- **Villagers**: talk to the locals for tips about the island, its boss and its bar.
- **Reverse Mountain**: after you beat Smoker in Logue Town, the sea chart lets you cross the Red Line. The current carries your ship UP the mountain and drops it down the waterfall into the Grand Line.
- **Awakening**: much later in the game, your Devil Fruit can awaken (Gear 5 for the Rubber fruit, Awakened Dough, Awakened Strings, and so on). You need 300 fruit mastery, level 55, Conqueror's Haki, and to have beaten Admiral Akainu at Marineford. Then the Gear menu offers the Awakening Trial: a solo fight against your fruit's own Will. Win it, and in battle you can **Awaken** for 3 turns: you hit 40% harder, take 25% less damage, change color, and can use two ultimate moves.
- **Moves**: every move has its own animation and calls its name in a banner, and a weapon's plain attack shows that weapon swinging, stabbing, smashing or firing.

Eighteen Devil Fruits are hidden across the islands, shuffled every new game: Rubber, Flame, Frost, Quake, Smoke, Light, Dough, Dragon, Split, Magma, String, Paw, Bird-Bird: Phoenix, Op-Op, Dark-Dark, Rumble-Rumble, Sand-Sand and Flower-Flower. Each has its own swirly shape (the Op-Op Fruit is a heart, String and Flower grow in bunches). Phoenix heals 5% of your HP every turn and Flames of Rebirth heals you as it burns; Op-Op gives +3 SP every turn and its Shambles never misses; Dark-Dark gives +10% defense and Black Hole drains HP; Rumble and Sand are Logias that attacks sometimes pass through (El Thor stuns, Desert Spada weakens); Flower-Flower adds 6% dodge and Clutch never misses. The Dragon fruit makes you half human, half azure dragon for good (horns, scales and a tail), turns you fully into the dragon when you use its moves, and gives +20% max HP (a crewmate you feed it turns half dragon too); Dough takes 12% less damage, Rubber and Paw 10% less; Light, Flame, Magma and Split let attacks miss you sometimes. Magma burns, String's Puppet Strings and Paw's Pressure Repel can stun. A fruit you keep instead of eating can be fed to a crewmate at the bar. Train Haki to unlock Armament (hits Logias), Observation (dodge) and Conqueror's Will. The Coral Trident's Riptide and Water Barrels also make Logias hittable, and the Night Edge and Smoker's Seastone Jitte hit them directly.

## Controls

Tap or click the buttons. Tap your pirate to chat. Space bar strikes in training and reels in fishing. Tap the message box to skip ahead.

## Code

- `js/gfx.js`: ASCII pixel sprites with auto-outlines, a 3x5 bitmap font, pixel drawing helpers
- `js/scene.js`: island backdrops, day/night skies, animated sea, your customizable ship and its deck, the Reverse Mountain cut-scene, particles and tweens
- `js/data.js`: islands, enemies, bosses, crew roles and recruits, devil fruits, items
- `js/audio.js`: chiptune sound effects with Web Audio
- `js/gear.js`: weapons, devil fruits, move mastery, exploring and the Gear menu
- `js/moves.js`: the animation for every move and every weapon's plain attack
- `js/moves-islands.js`: animations for the weapons from Water 7 to Whole Cake Island
- `js/moves-awaken.js`: the awakening transformation and every awakened move
- `js/game.js`: game state, menus, the day loop, battles, raids and mini-games
